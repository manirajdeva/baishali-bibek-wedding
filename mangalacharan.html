Good to Know

What is Mangalacharan?

Mangalacharan is an auspicious invocation – prayers and blessings offered to begin the wedding celebrations on a joyful note.

Where is it held?

At Infinity Hall, Mayflower Hotel, Panbazar, Guwahati.

What should I wear?

Traditional attire is welcome – ivory, gold, maroon or olive tones.
